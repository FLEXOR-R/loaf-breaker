- **KICK › DIST › PRE EQ**: three bands (LOW, MID, HIGH, their frequencies and the MID's Q) on the kick *before* its
  drive and DISTOs: the same DISTO gives another kick.
- **KICK › DIST › SUB SPLIT**: X FREQ splits the kick; its lows go around the drive and the DISTOs and come back clean
  (or with their own SUB DIRT), with their own SUB LEVEL and SUB DECAY. Drawn in frequency: the split a white line.
- KICK › DIRT is now DIST; its tabs shortened (DIST, SCR, SFX ..) so the ten fit.
- The KICK and drum pages: the knobs' cards take the place of the knobs' line at the foot (it said the same), the
  black keys' functions are no longer shown there, and the picture is half as tall again.
- Every page's foot: each knob a card (its name, its value) with a fader under it when the setting has a travel (the
  levels, the filters from their middle, the FX, GENERATOR, CHAOS, ARP's amount ..); a list or a mode, no fader.
