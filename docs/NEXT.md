- **LOAF LORD**: a new screen effect (SYSTEM › SCREEN FX, white key 8). LOAF LORDS rain down the screen, bounce once on its bottom and fall through it. SCREEN SET's AMOUNT sets the steady rain, ON A KICK a loaf on the kicks, AT THE DROP a burst.
- **PRE EQ in three bands**: KICK › DIST's PRE EQ is now **PRE L**, **PRE M** and **PRE H**, each band its own tab with K1 **DB**, K2 **FREQ** and K3 **Q**: the low and high bands get a Q too, so you can carve sharp peaks or notches wherever you want before the distortion.
- **The layers' effects beside their layer**: SFX, PFX, GFX and CFX moved out of DIST, each right after its layer (ATTACK: CLICK, CFX, PUNCH, PFX; BODY: SUB, SFX, GRIT, GFX).
- **The Studio in the VST's look**: the site's Studio (editor/) now looks like the LOAF BREAKER VST, black and bone: its header (PLAY, TEMPO, BREAK, PATTERN, the master live), the eight categories (LIVE, SOUNDS, PATTERN, SONG, MIX, EFFECTS, ARP, SYSTEM) and their tabs, every setting a card with a value bar, the pads at the foot, the VST's skins. It still runs on the FM-1's own power, over USB; to edit and to work on LOAF BREAKER, the VST is far better, and the Studio says so.
