- **MIX › FILTERS**, a new tab on the mixer (LFO, then SELECT): K1 the break filter's kind (CLASSIC as before, SMOOTH like a DJ mixer, SOFT a gentle 6 dB, RESO with a peak), K2 the drum filter's kind, K3 RESO (a resonance on both filters, 0 % = as before), K4 KICK SWITCH. The drum filter's kind and KICK SWITCH move there from SYSTEM › SCREEN FX.
- **909 drums**: a new FM-1 (and RESET DRUMS) now starts on a 909 kit, chosen by ear: the 909 snare (185 Hz, brighter), 909 CH (a metallic closed hat, the CLOSED preset before) and the 909 clap; 909 OH (the OPEN preset before) is the open hat. Your saved drums don't change: pick the presets to hear them.
- **HH and OH**: each hat step can now be closed (HH) or open (OH). On SEQ, hold a step of the HAT lane and turn K1 (right open, left closed); an open step is drawn hollow. The open hat is the hat's sound with a longer tail, set on the hat's SPACE page (K1 OH DECAY, K2 OH LEVEL); the next hat cuts it off, as on a 909. MIDI note 46 plays it open. The screens say HAT instead of HI-HAT.
