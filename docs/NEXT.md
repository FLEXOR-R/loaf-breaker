- **Fine tempo and tap tempo** (as on CHOPPAH): SYSTEM › MIDI, **K4 BPM FINE TUNE** moves it by 0.1 BPM (SELECT stays 1 BPM);
  on that page **any white key taps the tempo** (from the second tap; 2 s without one starts again).
- **Drums on the black keys**: four new functions, **KCK, SNR, HAT, CLP** (ARP › BLACK, K3; or SEL BLACK, or the REC
  key): a press plays that drum, and with REC on while playing it is written into its lane. Finger-drum from HOME.
