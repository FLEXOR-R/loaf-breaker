- Four new black-key functions to play the drums live: **KDR** turns the snare, hi-hat and clap off (and back on with the next press), **KSN**, **KHH** and **KCP** do it for one drum each. The kicks keep playing; the key stays lit while its drums are off. Put them on a key in ARP › BLACK (CUSTOM), SEL BLACK or REC.
