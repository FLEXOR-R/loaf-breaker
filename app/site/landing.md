# LOAF BREAKER

LOAF BREAKER turns the M-VAVE FM-1 into a hardcore kick synthesizer powerhouse and a breakbeat chopper, by FLEXOR.
Built for hardcore, gabber, terror and speedcore: shape a heavy kick from its attack to its tail, with its own synth,
DISTO and SCREAM distortion, its own effects and twenty kick memories, and bend it live from the knobs. Chop a break on
the white keys, throw effects with the black keys, program the kick, snare, hat and clap step by step, then chain
patterns into tracks with builds and drops. BEGINNER keeps it simple, EXPERT opens everything, and a tutorial gets you
started on the FM-1 itself.

Version {{VERSION}} (beta). Installing is at your own risk; the installer can put M-VAVE's own firmware back. 10 breaks, 2 bars
each: AMEN20 164, AMEN19 172, UPDOWN 165, THINK 114, AKIRA 190, DETEST 175, LOUDER 175, SATAN 200, PANTHER 170, OBEDIENC165. Five to seven of your own after them: [Breaks](breaks/), the seventy breaks of the library to hear, or a sample of yours.

- [Emulator](emu/) The same code the FM-1 runs, in the browser, with sound. No FM-1 needed.
- [Install](install/) From Chrome or Edge, with the FM-1 connected by USB.
- [Manual](manual/) Start in five minutes, then the kick, the sequencer, the tracks and drops, the pads, the effects.
- [Controls](manual/#controls) The control map: every page's buttons and knobs, to explore.
- [Breaks](breaks/) The seventy breaks of the library to hear, and yours sent to the FM-1.
- [Source](https://github.com/FLEXOR-R/loaf-breaker) The code (MPL-2.0) and every version's .fwsc.
