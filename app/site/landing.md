# LOAF BREAKER

LOAF BREAKER turns the M-VAVE FM-1 into a hardcore and breakcore break chopper, by FLEXOR. Chop a break on the white
keys, throw effects with the black keys, and shape a heavy kick with its own synth and distortion. Program the kick,
snare, hat and clap step by step, then chain patterns into tracks with drops. BEGINNER keeps it simple, EXPERT opens
everything, and a tutorial gets you started on the FM-1 itself.

Version {{VERSION}} (beta). Installing is at your own risk; the installer can put M-VAVE's own firmware back. 9 breaks, 2 bars
each: NUSKOOL 140, AMEN20 164, AMEN19 172, UPDOWN 165, AMEN15 174, JUNGLE 175, THINK 114, APACHE 115, THEWORM 110.

- [Try it in the browser](emu/): the same code the FM-1 runs, with sound. No FM-1 needed.
- [Install](install/): from Chrome or Edge, with the FM-1 connected by USB.
- [Manual](manual/): start in five minutes, then the kick, the sequencer, the tracks and drops, the pads, the effects.
- [Source and releases](https://github.com/FLEXOR-R/loaf-breaker): the code (MPL-2.0) and every version's .fwsc.
