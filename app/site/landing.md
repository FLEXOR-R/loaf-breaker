# LOAF BREAKER

LOAF BREAKER turns the M-VAVE FM-1 into a hardcore kick synthesizer powerhouse and a breakbeat chopper, by FLEXOR.
Built for hardcore, gabber, terror and speedcore: shape a heavy kick from its attack to its tail, with its own synth,
DISTO and SCREAM distortion, its own effects and twenty kick memories, and bend it live from the knobs. Chop a break on
the white keys, throw effects with the black keys, program the kick, snare, hat and clap step by step, then chain
patterns into tracks with builds and drops. BEGINNER keeps it simple, EXPERT opens everything, and a tutorial gets you
started on the FM-1 itself.

> **A work in progress, on purpose.** LOAF BREAKER changes all the time: new versions come out often, and each can move things. The built-in breaks are not final: they will be swapped for better ones as they are sampled (the breaks you send from [Breaks](breaks/) stay yours). Pages of the firmware and of this site may still change their layout or their look, and some sections will be redesigned. The aim is a machine that is quick to play live, easy to find your way in, and yours to shape: if you are here, you are early, and the best is still to come.

Version {{VERSION}} (beta). Installing is at your own risk; the installer can put M-VAVE's own firmware back. 9 breaks, 2 bars
each: BANGPHS 155, AMEN20 164, UPDOWN 165, AMEN19 172, PANTHER 170, LOUDER 175, FOCUZ 175, SHUFFLE 190, SATAN 200. Five to seven of your own after them: [Breaks](breaks/), the library's breaks to hear, or a sample of yours.

- [Emulator](emu/) The same code the FM-1 runs, in the browser, with sound. No FM-1 needed.
- [Install](install/) From Chrome or Edge, with the FM-1 connected by USB.
- [Manual](manual/) Start in five minutes, then the kick, the sequencer, the tracks and drops, the pads, the effects.
- [Controls](manual/#controls) The control map: every page's buttons and knobs, to explore.
- [Studio](editor/) The whole FM-1 on the computer, as a DAW: the kick and the drums with every setting at once, the sequencer, the 64 patterns, the tracks of your set on a timeline, the effects and the mix, the waveforms in RGB and the mix scrolling live (USB, Chrome or Edge).
- [Mirror](mirror/) The FM-1's screen and panel live on your computer over USB (Chrome or Edge): see it big, record it, play the panel from the computer.
- [Breaks](breaks/) The library's breaks to hear (over ninety, FLEXOR's hardcore breaks among them), and yours sent to the FM-1.
- [Source](https://github.com/FLEXOR-R/loaf-breaker) The code (MPL-2.0) and every version's .fwsc.
