Start in 5 minutes

Your first five minutes

LOAF BREAKER is on your FM-1? These five steps are all you need to start. The FM-1 begins in BEGINNER mode, which keeps the advanced pages out of the way until you want them.

Or let the FM-1 show you. A new FM-1 asks at its first start: TUTORIAL, BEGINNER or EXPERT (turn a knob, PLAY to choose). The tutorial tells you one thing at a time on the screen and blinks the button to press. You play the real firmware the whole way. GLO leaves it, and SYSTEM › SETUP K3 starts it again.

Press PLAY

A breakbeat starts with a kick under it. ALGORITHM picks another break (it changes at the next bar), SELECT sets the tempo, PRESETS another pattern. PLAY again stops.

Play the white keys

The 16 white keys are the break's 16 slices. Tap one to play that slice, hold one to loop it in time until you let go. Play two or three in turn: you're already chopping.

Hold a black key

The black keys are effects: they work while you hold them, and you can hold several. Rolls, reverse, freeze, half speed, a vinyl stop, gate, filter, echo… While one is held, K1–K4 tune it.

EDIT the kick

EDIT opens the kick, drawn big. K1–K4 change what's on screen, ALGORITHM goes to the next part, SELECT to the next section. Start with a ready-made kick (909, Rotterdam, Gabber, Terror…), then add DISTO to make it hard. PRESETS holds your twenty kicks, each with its own sound.

Build, drop, write

SEL + ENV: a build-up from the next bar, then the drop. Then write your own beats on SEQ (the white keys are the steps, ALGORITHM picks the lane) and chain them into a set on ENV.

Three things to remember.

SEL + K1–K4, on any page: four knobs that change the kick while it plays. Press SEL twice to lock it and keep both hands free.

SEL tapped alone shows the shortcuts of the page you're on. A page's button pressed again takes you back HOME.

SAVE keeps everything (or turn on AUTO SAVE in SYSTEM › SETUP).

When you know your way around, switch to EXPERT (SYSTEM › SETUP, K3) to see every page. The manual covers the rest.
