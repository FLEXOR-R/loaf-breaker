THE STUDIO drives your FM-1 over USB: every sound and every step is the machine's own, worked out by its small processor, a setting at a time. THE LOAF BREAKER VST runs the whole firmware on your computer: a full-size sequencer and arranger, EQ curves you drag, every setting of a page at once, 128 breaks, no wait, in your DAW, and it can still send everything to the FM-1. To edit and to work on LOAF BREAKER, the VST is far better.
