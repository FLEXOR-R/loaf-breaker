Your FM-1, from a screen

Four ways to see and drive the FM-1 while it's plugged in. All of them work over USB, in Chrome or Edge; nothing to install.

On a computerStudioEvery setting of the FM-1 on a big screen, in the VST's look: the kick and the drums, the sequencer, your patterns and songs, the effects and the mix. On a phoneLiveThe FM-1's screen and all its buttons, keys and knobs in your hand, on a phone plugged in by USB. To film or streamMirrorThe FM-1's screen, big and live on your computer, to record it or show it. You can play its panel from there too. On AndroidAppAll of this on your phone's home screen, as an app: Live, Studio, the breaks, your drum samples, the updates.

Want to edit for real? The VST is far better. The same LOAF BREAKER on your computer, with every setting at once, a big sequencer and a song arranger, and your work sent to the FM-1 when you're done.
