Breaks

The FM-1 plays the breaks built into LOAF BREAKER, and your own after them on ALGORITHM: 232 KB of room, five to seven breaks of the library's size (a break takes 29 to 47 KB). Hear the seventy breaks of the project's library, or a sample of yours chopped exactly as the FM-1 will play it, add the ones you want to your list, and send it over USB. Chrome or Edge, on a computer.

1. The FM-1

Your breaks

LOAF BREAKER must be stopped while it is written: writing the flash pauses the sound. Sending replaces what is on the FM-1 with your list as it is here (an empty list deletes them). Your breaks go in two places of the flash (the two bars: 140 KB and 92 KB); the page picks each one's and rewrites only a place that changes.

2. Listening:

Click a slice, or press 1–8 and Q–I, to hear it alone. Chop plays the slices in a new order every bar, the way the generator rearranges a break. What you hear is the break as the FM-1 holds it.

3. The library

Every break of the project, made as the built-in ones: two bars, levelled, sixteen slices on the hits.
