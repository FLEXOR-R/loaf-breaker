LOAF BREAKER

Your FM-1, from your phone

LiveThe FM-1's screen and its whole panel under your fingers: the keys, the pads, the knobs, Drop. StudioThe kick, the drums, the sequencer, patterns, tracks, effects. BreaksHear the library, send your own breaks to the FM-1. ImportYour own snare, hat and clap as samples. ManualEvery page and every control.

Connect: Chrome on Android, the FM-1 plugged into the phone with a USB-C cable (an OTG one if the phone asks). LOAF BREAKER has no Bluetooth. Each page has a Connect button: allow MIDI when Chrome asks.

Install: Chrome's menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen). It opens full screen, like an app, and updates itself with the site.
